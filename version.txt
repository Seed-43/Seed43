Last update:

  Disclaimer:
  - Seed43 tools are under active development and provided as-is.

  New
  - 3D Import - one button for every 3D format: steel IFCs (Tekla,
    ProStructures) and StruCad .bsw files come in as native framing,
    columns and braces, Revit steel plates with their cuts, and bolts
    and anchors; .seed files from Seed43 Studio, 3D Seed and Navisworks
    too. Grids and levels come with them (replaces Steel IFC)
  - Section Map - match a file's sections to your family types, import
    them as shapes or plates, and keep the choices for later imports
  - 3D Seed - exports a smart model for Seed43 Studio: an IFC with every
    Revit parameter
  - Sheet Scale - writes viewport scales onto "As indicated" sheets

  General
  - 3D PDF, 3D Seed and 3D Import moved to the 3D Tools panel slide-out
  - Repeated custom steel parts become editable Generic Model families
  - Saved settings fully moved to .user; the old migration code is gone

  Updated
  - pySheets - new 3D PDF format for 3D views
  - pyTransmit - Schedule Pages card (ignore page height, distribution
    on every page, split after sheets); Studio gets a Structure panel,
    Undo/Redo buttons, a status readout and font sizes in mm/in; the
    old Layout Builder is removed
  - pyLink - schedules built at Excel's own sizes, with a scale per card

  Fixes
  - pyTransmit Studio: short rows clipping their text
  - pyLink: merged titles losing their text size, and column widths
    lost on refit
